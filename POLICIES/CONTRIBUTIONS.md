# Contributions Policy

**ZeroControlStudios**
Independent Development Studio
France

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes general principles for contributions to ZeroControlStudios projects.

Individual projects may impose additional requirements.

## 2. Authorization

Only authorized persons may contribute directly to private ZeroControlStudios repositories.

Access to a repository does not automatically grant permission to access unrelated projects or infrastructure.

## 3. Contribution Standards

Contributions should:

* comply with the project's technical requirements;
* respect applicable licenses;
* avoid unauthorized third-party material;
* avoid malicious code;
* avoid intentionally introducing security vulnerabilities;
* and follow project-specific contribution guidelines.

## 4. Intellectual Property

Contributing code, documentation, assets, or other material does not by itself establish the ownership or licensing terms applicable to that contribution.

Those rights are determined by:

* applicable law;
* the contributor's legal status;
* the project's license;
* and any applicable written agreement.

## 5. Pre-Existing Material

Contributors must identify relevant pre-existing material before incorporating it into a ZeroControlStudios project where its ownership or licensing conditions could affect the project.

## 6. Confidential Projects

Contributions to private projects must be treated according to applicable confidentiality requirements.

Private source code and internal project information must not be publicly disclosed without authorization.

## 7. Project-Specific Rules

Individual repositories may contain additional:

* contribution guidelines;
* coding standards;
* licensing requirements;
* security requirements;
* review procedures;
* or other project-specific rules.

Those rules apply to the relevant project.
