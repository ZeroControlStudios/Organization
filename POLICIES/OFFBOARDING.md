# Offboarding Policy

**ZeroControlStudios**
Independent Development Studio
France

## 1. Purpose

This policy describes the general security and operational process used when a person's access to ZeroControlStudios projects or systems ends.

The applicable contractual or legal agreement remains controlling where it contains additional requirements.

## 2. Access Revocation

When access is no longer required, ZeroControlStudios may revoke or modify access to:

* GitHub repositories;
* development systems;
* servers;
* databases;
* cloud services;
* communication systems;
* project management tools;
* and other ZeroControlStudios resources.

## 3. Return of Materials

Where applicable, materials belonging to or provided by ZeroControlStudios must be returned in accordance with the applicable agreement or legal requirements.

This may include:

* source code;
* project files;
* documentation;
* assets;
* databases;
* credentials;
* keys;
* devices;
* and other project materials.

## 4. Copies of Confidential Material

Where required by the applicable agreement, confidential or proprietary ZeroControlStudios material must no longer be retained after the end of the relevant collaboration, subject to legally required retention or other applicable legal exceptions.

This may include copies stored on:

* personal computers;
* external drives;
* personal cloud services;
* removable media;
* or other personal storage locations.

## 5. Credentials

Credentials and authentication information provided for ZeroControlStudios systems must not be retained or reused after access has been revoked.

Where appropriate, credentials may be rotated following an individual's departure.

## 6. Work in Progress

Where applicable, work created during a collaboration must be returned or transferred according to the terms governing that collaboration.

The technical transfer of work does not itself determine ownership or intellectual property rights.

## 7. Continuing Obligations

Ending access to ZeroControlStudios systems does not automatically terminate obligations that legally or contractually continue after the end of a collaboration, including applicable confidentiality or intellectual property obligations.
