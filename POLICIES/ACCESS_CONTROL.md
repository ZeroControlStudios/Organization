# Access Control Policy

**ZeroControlStudios**
Independent Development Studio
France

## 1. Principle of Least Privilege

Access to ZeroControlStudios systems should be limited to the minimum level required for a person's role and responsibilities.

Access to one project does not automatically imply access to other projects.

## 2. Individual Accounts

Access should be provided through individual accounts whenever possible.

Shared accounts should be avoided.

Each user is responsible for protecting the credentials associated with their account.

## 3. Project-Based Access

Permissions may be assigned according to:

* organization role;
* project;
* repository;
* environment;
* infrastructure component;
* or specific operational responsibility.

## 4. Administrative Access

Administrative privileges should be restricted to authorized personnel whose responsibilities require them.

Administrative access should not be granted merely for convenience.

## 5. Production Access

Production environments should be separated from development environments whenever reasonably possible.

Access to production systems should be limited and controlled.

## 6. Access Review

Access may be reviewed periodically and may be modified or revoked when it is no longer required.

## 7. Revocation

ZeroControlStudios may immediately revoke or restrict access where necessary for:

* security;
* operational continuity;
* account compromise;
* role changes;
* project changes;
* or termination of a collaboration.

Access revocation does not by itself determine or modify any intellectual property rights or contractual obligations.
