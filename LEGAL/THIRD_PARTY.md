**ZeroControlStudios**

## 1. General Principle

ZeroControlStudios projects may use software, libraries, frameworks, assets, services, documentation, trademarks, and other materials created or owned by third parties.

Such materials remain subject to the rights and licenses of their respective owners.

## 2. No Ownership Claim

The inclusion, use, distribution, or reference of third-party material within a ZeroControlStudios project does not by itself transfer ownership of that material to ZeroControlStudios.

## 3. Third-Party Licenses

Each third-party component must be used in accordance with its applicable license or terms.

Where required, applicable copyright notices, license texts, attribution notices, or other legal notices should be retained and distributed with the relevant project.

## 4. Dependencies

Developers and contributors should verify the licensing conditions of dependencies before introducing them into a project.

Particular attention should be given to:

* copyleft licenses;
* commercial-use restrictions;
* attribution requirements;
* redistribution requirements;
* asset licenses;
* proprietary SDKs;
* and restrictions incompatible with the intended distribution model.

## 5. Trademarks

Third-party trademarks remain the property of their respective owners.

The use of a third-party trademark does not imply endorsement, partnership, or affiliation unless explicitly stated.

## 6. Contributor Responsibility

A contributor must not knowingly introduce third-party material into a ZeroControlStudios project without having the necessary rights or permissions to do so.

Where licensing uncertainty exists, the material should be reviewed before inclusion.

## 7. Project-Specific Notices

Individual repositories may contain a `NOTICE`, `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES`, or equivalent file identifying third-party components and their applicable licenses.

Those notices apply to the relevant project.
