# Confidentiality Policy

**ZeroControlStudios**
Independent Development Studio
France

## 1. Purpose

ZeroControlStudios protects information that is not publicly available and that may have commercial, technical, operational, or strategic value.

This policy describes the general confidentiality principles applicable to ZeroControlStudios projects and activities.

## 2. Confidential Information

Depending on the circumstances, confidential information may include:

* private source code;
* private repositories;
* unpublished software;
* unreleased projects;
* internal documentation;
* technical architecture;
* infrastructure information;
* database structures;
* credentials;
* API keys;
* authentication information;
* security configurations;
* development tools and internal systems;
* business information;
* financial information;
* unreleased product information;
* roadmaps;
* internal communications;
* user or customer information;
* and other non-public information designated or reasonably understood to be confidential.

## 3. Public Information

Information that has been intentionally and officially published by ZeroControlStudios is generally not considered confidential solely because it was previously internal.

However, publication of one part of a project does not automatically make related private information public.

## 4. Use of Confidential Information

Confidential information may only be accessed and used for legitimate ZeroControlStudios purposes and within the scope of the access granted to the relevant person.

Confidential information must not be:

* disclosed to unauthorized persons;
* copied for unauthorized purposes;
* published;
* sold;
* redistributed;
* used for unrelated projects;
* or otherwise exploited without authorization.

## 5. Credentials and Security Information

Credentials, authentication tokens, API keys, private keys, passwords, and similar security information must be treated as confidential.

They must not be committed to public repositories or intentionally disclosed to unauthorized persons.

## 6. Continuing Obligations

Confidentiality obligations may continue after a person's access to ZeroControlStudios systems or projects ends, subject to applicable law and any applicable agreement.

## 7. Agreements

Where a formal confidentiality agreement or NDA is required, the applicable agreement governs the specific relationship and may impose additional obligations.

This policy does not replace such an agreement.
