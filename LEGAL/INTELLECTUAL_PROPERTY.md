# Intellectual Property Policy

**ZeroControlStudios**
Independent Development Studio
France

## 1. Purpose

This policy describes the general principles governing intellectual property associated with ZeroControlStudios and its projects.

It is intended to provide a clear public framework. It does not replace an individual employment agreement, contractor agreement, contributor agreement, assignment of rights, or other legally binding agreement where such an agreement is required.

## 2. ZeroControlStudios Works

Subject to applicable law and any third-party rights, ZeroControlStudios may own or hold rights in works created by the studio, including:

* software;
* source code;
* scripts;
* tools;
* documentation;
* graphical assets;
* interfaces;
* designs;
* audiovisual material;
* project documentation;
* branding;
* and other original works.

## 3. Contributions

A person contributing to a ZeroControlStudios project may retain rights in their contribution unless applicable law or a valid agreement provides otherwise.

Participation in a project, access to a repository, employment, contracting relationship, or contribution to a project must not be interpreted by itself as creating or transferring intellectual property rights beyond what is provided by applicable law or an applicable agreement.

Where ZeroControlStudios requires ownership or specific exploitation rights over contributions, the applicable rights and conditions should be established in the appropriate written agreement.

## 4. Employee-Created Software

Where applicable, the specific legal rules governing software created by employees apply.

In particular, French intellectual property law contains specific provisions concerning the economic rights in software and its documentation created by employees in the course of their duties or according to their employer's instructions.

The applicable legal framework and the employee's contractual relationship determine the rights applicable to a particular situation.

## 5. Independent Contractors and External Contributors

Independent contractors, external developers, contributors, and other third parties may be subject to different intellectual property rules.

ZeroControlStudios does not assume that intellectual property rights are automatically transferred merely because a person has contributed to a project.

Where a transfer, assignment, license, or other grant of rights is required, it should be established through an appropriate written agreement.

## 6. Pre-Existing Works

A contributor should not introduce pre-existing code, assets, libraries, documentation, or other protected material into a ZeroControlStudios project unless they have the necessary rights and the use is compatible with the project's requirements and applicable licenses.

Pre-existing works remain subject to their original ownership and licensing conditions unless expressly agreed otherwise.

## 7. Project Licenses

Each project may define its own licensing model.

Possible models include:

* proprietary / all rights reserved;
* source-available;
* open-source;
* third-party licensed software;
* or another specifically defined licensing arrangement.

The license attached to an individual project governs that project.

## 8. Enforcement

ZeroControlStudios reserves all rights and remedies available under applicable law in relation to unauthorized use, reproduction, distribution, disclosure, or exploitation of its protected works.
